# Insight AI

### Turning Customer Feedback into Actionable Product Decisions

Insight AI is an open-source, AI-powered product intelligence platform designed to help product teams understand customer feedback, identify recurring problems, discover product opportunities, and make evidence-backed product decisions.

Product teams receive feedback from multiple sources, including customer support tickets, app reviews, surveys, user interviews, and feature requests. However, extracting meaningful insights from fragmented and unstructured information remains challenging.

Insight AI aims to bridge this gap by transforming customer signals into structured insights that support better product discovery, prioritisation, and decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Vision

To help product teams move beyond collecting customer feedback and start making smarter, evidence-driven product decisions.

Insight AI aims to connect customer problems with product opportunities through intelligent feedback analysis, explainable AI, and structured prioritisation.

## 🚀 Key Capabilities

The following are proposed product capabilities and will be validated and developed incrementally.

- **Feedback Management:** Import, organise, and categorise customer feedback from multiple sources.
- **AI-Powered Analysis:** Identify recurring themes, customer pain points, sentiment, and feature requests.
- **Product Intelligence:** Discover emerging trends, frequently reported issues, and potential product opportunities.
- **Evidence-Based Insights:** Connect AI-generated insights to their underlying customer feedback records.
- **Feature Prioritisation:** Evaluate opportunities using frameworks such as RICE, impact-effort analysis, and customer problem severity.
- **AI Product Assistant:** Ask questions about customer feedback and generate evidence-backed product insights.
- **Product Documentation:** Generate initial problem statements, opportunity briefs, user stories, and acceptance criteria for human review.
- **Insight Reporting:** Create structured reports to communicate findings and support product decisions.

## 🧠 Product Philosophy

Insight AI is being developed around a few fundamental principles:

1. **Evidence over assumptions:** Insights should be supported by actual customer feedback.
2. **AI with purpose:** Use AI where it provides measurable value, not simply because it is available.
3. **Explainability:** Make it clear how insights are generated and what evidence supports them.
4. **Human-in-the-loop:** Product managers retain control over prioritisation and final decisions.
5. **Transparency:** Communicate uncertainty and avoid presenting generated hypotheses as verified facts.
6. **Privacy by design:** Protect customer information through appropriate data handling and access controls.

## 🔍 Guiding Success Metrics

The platform will be evaluated using both technical and product-oriented metrics:

- Feedback categorisation accuracy.
- Theme identification quality.
- Percentage of insights supported by source evidence.
- AI hallucination rate.
- Time required to analyse customer feedback.
- Time required to prepare product opportunity briefs.
- User acceptance of AI-generated insights.
- Overall product usefulness and adoption.
